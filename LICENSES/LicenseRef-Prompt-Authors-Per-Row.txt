LicenseRef-Prompt-Authors-Per-Row

The prompt bodies and titles in this repository were written by the individual
people credited in ATTRIBUTION.md, not by Formative Intelligence Inc.

Each prompt retains the licence terms stated by the collection through which
this repository received it. The authoritative record is the
`upstreamStatedTerms` field on that prompt's row in data/prompts.json:

    CC0-1.0    Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
    CC-BY-4.0  Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International

ATTRIBUTION.md renders the author, original post, source collection and stated
terms for every prompt. Copies of the upstream licence statements, pinned to
the commits used, are in provenance/.

Formative Intelligence Inc. does not claim authorship and grants no additional
rights in third-party work. Reusers must follow the terms recorded for the
specific prompt and preserve any attribution those terms require.

If you wrote a prompt and want it removed, see TAKEDOWN.md.
